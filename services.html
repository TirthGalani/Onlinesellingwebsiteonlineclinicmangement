Our treatments

Considered dentistry for every stage of life.

From routine prevention to complete smile restoration, every treatment is planned around your comfort, your goals and the long-term health of your teeth.

General Cosmetic Orthodontics Restorative Pediatric Emergency
What we offer

Five pillars of complete care.

Each service below includes what is involved, the benefits you can expect and a typical appointment time. Your exact plan will always be confirmed after examination.

01 · Preventive care

General Dentistry

Routine care is the foundation of a healthy smile. Our general appointments include a gentle examination, professional hygiene, oral cancer screening and clear advice on home care. If a filling is needed, we use tooth-coloured materials and preserve as much natural tooth as possible.

Expected time30–60 minutes
RecallEvery 6 months
  • Early detection of decay, gum disease and oral health changes.
  • Fresher breath and a smoother, cleaner smile.
  • Conservative, tooth-coloured fillings where treatment is needed.
Book General Care
02 · Aesthetic dentistry

Cosmetic Dentistry

Professional whitening that brightens your smile safely and effectively, with a shade plan matched to your natural teeth. We also offer porcelain veneers, composite bonding and digital smile design for shape, proportion and subtle refinement — never an artificial look.

Whitening60–90 minutes
Veneers2–3 visits
  • Natural-looking results with careful shade and shape matching.
  • Minimally invasive techniques that preserve healthy enamel.
  • Digital previews so you can see the plan before treatment begins.
Book a Smile Consultation
03 · Alignment

Orthodontics

Clear aligners and modern braces can improve crowding, spacing and bite function at almost any age. We begin with a digital assessment, explain the realistic movement plan and give you a clear timeline before you commit to treatment.

Consultation30–45 minutes
Treatment6–24 months
  • Discreet clear aligner options for adults and older teens.
  • Digital planning and progress reviews at each stage.
  • Retainers and follow-up care to protect your result.
Book an Orthodontic Assessment
04 · Rebuild & restore

Restorative Dentistry

When a tooth is damaged or missing, our restorative care focuses on strength, function and a natural appearance. Options include crowns, bridges, implants and endodontic treatment, planned with digital imaging and explained step by step.

Appointment60–120 minutes
Implant journey3–6 months
  • Restores comfortable chewing and protects weakened teeth.
  • Replaces missing teeth without compromising neighbouring teeth.
  • Natural-looking ceramics matched to your existing smile.
Book a Restorative Consultation
05 · Young smiles

Pediatric Dentistry

Children deserve a gentle introduction to dentistry. We use clear, age-appropriate explanations, a playful pace and preventive care such as sealants and fluoride. Parents leave with practical guidance, not judgement.

Expected time30–45 minutes
First visitFrom age 1
  • A positive, confidence-building first experience.
  • Preventive sealants, fluoride and dietary guidance.
  • Early assessment of spacing, crowding and bite development.
Book a Children's Visit
How it works

Simple, transparent care planning.

1

Consultation
