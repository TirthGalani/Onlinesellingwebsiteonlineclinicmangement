First visit guide

Arrive as you are. We'll take it from here.

A first appointment is a conversation as much as an examination. We will ask about your health, your concerns and what you would like to change. Then we examine gently, explain what we see and agree on the next step together.

  1. 1

    Book your time

    Request an appointment online or call. Tell us if you feel nervous or need extra time.

  2. 2

    Arrive 10 minutes early

    Complete your medical history and meet your clinician. Bring your ID and insurance card.

  3. 3

    Consultation & examination

    We listen, examine and may take digital scans or X-rays. Everything is explained as we go.

  4. 4

    Your written plan

    You leave with clear options, expected times and fees. Nothing proceeds without your consent.

What to bring

  • Photo identification.
  • Your dental insurance card, if you have one.
  • A list of medications, supplements and allergies.
  • Any previous dental records or X-rays you can access.
  • Your questions — written down is fine.
Allow 45–60 minutes New patient appointments include a full examination, so please leave a little extra time.

Insurance & payment

Clear fees. No surprises.

We believe you should know the cost before treatment begins. After your examination you receive a written estimate, and we are happy to explain every item. We work with most major insurers and can help you understand your benefits before you commit.

Dental insurance

We work with most major providers. Bring your card and we will help verify benefits and submit claims where supported.

Flexible payment

Card and bank payments are accepted. For larger plans, ask about staged payments or third-party financing, subject to approval.

Written estimates

Every recommended treatment comes with a written plan and fee estimate, so you can decide without pressure.

Good to know

  • Exact fees are confirmed after your examination and any required imaging.
  • Insurance cover varies by plan; we can help you interpret your benefits.
  • Payment plans and financing are subject to provider approval.
  • You will never be asked to commit to treatment on the day of your consultation.
This page provides general guidance only. Your written estimate and the clinic's current fee schedule take precedence.

Before & after care

General guidance for a comfortable recovery.

The instructions below are general. Your clinician may give you specific written advice for your treatment — always follow that first. If something feels wrong, call us rather than guessing.

Before your appointment

Please do

  • Eat normally unless we have told you sedation or a general anaesthetic is planned.
  • Take your regular prescribed medications unless your clinician advises otherwise.
  • Tell us if you are pregnant, breastfeeding or have a new medical condition.
  • Arrive 10 minutes early, especially for a first visit.
  • Tell us if you feel anxious — we can adapt the appointment.

Please avoid

  • Stopping prescribed medication without medical advice.
  • Smoking or alcohol before a surgical appointment.
  • Arriving without telling us about recent illness or fever.
  • Bringing children to an adult appointment unless arranged in advance.
  • Worrying alone — call us if you are unsure about anything.

After treatment

After a filling

  • Wait until numbness has gone before eating, to avoid biting your cheek or tongue.
  • Mild sensitivity to hot or cold is common for a few days.
  • Avoid very hard foods on the treated tooth for 24 hours.
  • Call us if the bite feels high or the pain worsens.

After an extraction

  • Bite gently on the gauze for 20–30 minutes as advised.
  • Do not rinse, spit or use a straw for 24 hours.
  • Use cold packs for swelling and take pain relief as directed.
  • Contact us if bleeding continues heavily or swelling increases.

After whitening

  • Avoid tea, coffee, red wine and dark sauces for 48 hours.
  • Temporary sensitivity usually settles within a few days.
  • Use the desensitising product recommended by your clinician.
  • Wait at least 24 hours before using any other whitening product.
If you are worried after treatment, call us. For severe bleeding, facial swelling, difficulty breathing or swallowing, seek urgent medical care immediately.
