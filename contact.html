Parking & landmarks

Plan a little extra time if you are driving — Midtown parking fills quickly on weekdays.

  • Nearest garage: 24-hour covered parking on East 50th Street, a two-minute walk from the clinic.
  • By subway: 5 minutes from 5th Avenue/53rd Street (E, M) and 51st Street (6).
  • Landmark: opposite the north-west corner of Madison Square Park, next door to the blue awning café.
  • Accessibility: step-free entrance, lift access and an accessible treatment room.
